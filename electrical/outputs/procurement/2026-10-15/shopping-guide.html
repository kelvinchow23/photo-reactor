Still needed outside this cart

  • Five printed reactor housings, with mounts for ten 55mm boards and heatsink retention. The original STLs need adaptation. This mechanical design is not finished; the hardware assortment is not a verified final enclosure fastener list.
  • Ten axially magnetized neodymium discs, 6mm diameter × 2mm thick. Two per stir fan. Manufacturer specification example. Use a small quantity supplier; this item is not in the DigiKey cart.
  • One small tube of suitable superglue for the stir-fan magnets, as in the original build. Keep adhesive out of the fan bearing and balance the pair.
  • At least five 8mL (2 dram) vials with suitable caps and five PTFE stir bars. Match vial outside dimensions to the printed holder and select stir bars that rotate freely inside the vial. Exact lab consumable selection remains open.

Lab tools are available, so no soldering iron, multimeter, stripper or heat gun is included. A computer with two USB-A ports is assumed. Firmware for the new controller must be commissioned; the original EMC2101-based software is not automatically compatible.

Wiring and hand assembly

Each reactor: controller port → intact 10-way cable → first LED board J1 IN. First board J2 OUT → custom link → second board J1 IN. Leave the second board J2 OUT empty. Connect one fan to each board J3.

Individually insulate second-IN wires 3, 4, 6 and 10. Map by connector contact numbers and continuity testing, not the apparent left/right position or wire colours. Each fan pigtail maps J3: 1 GND, 2 +5V, 3 tach, 4 PWM; confirm the fan lead functions against its datasheet. No crimp tool is required, but soldered insulated splices are required.

Before powering: Set each controller JP_FANV shunt to pins 2–3 (5V). The other position supplies 12V and can damage these fans. Check harness continuity and absence of shorts before connection.

Heatsink installation

The selected 21mm square sink fits within the 22mm rear copper contact and 26mm keepout. Use the insulating thermal sheet between it and the board. The LED thermal island is not guaranteed electrically isolated; anodizing alone is not adequate insulation. Cut pads to cover the entire contact footprint and retain the sink with a holder/clamp. The sheet's tack is not structural adhesive.

The sink is rated about 25.4°C/W in natural convection. At 0.8W this corresponds to about 20°C sink-to-air rise under the rating conditions, excluding PCB and interface resistance. This is a selection estimate, not validation of LED junction temperature. Check the assembled prototype at full output before operating all five unattended.
